## 👋 About Us

Channel Wise is an AI-powered platform that helps YouTube creators understand what's working, discover growth opportunities, and decide what to do next.

We believe creators shouldn't need an entire analytics team or expensive agency to make data-driven decisions.

Our mission is to make powerful creator intelligence accessible to everyone.

**Our philosophy:** Know what to try next. Know if it worked.

## 🛠️ Open Source

We build tools to solve real problems we encounter along the way, and share them with the developer community.

### 📬 Outreach Wise
An open-source, self-hosted cold email outreach tool featuring Google Sheets integration, A/B testing, automated follow-ups, and campaign analytics.
